## Start learning

New to PolicyOS? Begin with [What PolicyOS does](/learn/start-here/what-policyos-does). The ten-lesson guided course follows Avery Chen from employee facts to final assignments, a previewed change, an explicit override, and durable evidence.

## Find an answer

- **Core concepts** explain policies, versions, assignments, priority, effective dates, groups, overrides, approvals, and evidence.
- **How-to guides** give focused steps for real workflows.
- **Feature reference** describes the screens and controls you encounter.
- **Troubleshooting** gives you a diagnostic path when a result is unexpected.

## What you will be able to do

You will learn how to separate a policy match from the assignment it ultimately supplies, how to review impact before a change, and where to look when you need evidence about a past decision.

Need a definition while you read? Open the [Glossary](/learn/reference/glossary).

> The learning center uses fictional examples. Demo-mode changes are not persistent, and connected PolicyOS behavior remains authoritative.
