The **Users** tab shows account identity, employee link, explicit and policy-derived roles, effective permission count, password-change state, and status. Management authority enables create, edit, reset, and disable actions.

The **Roles** tab defines allow-only permission bundles. Employee scope controls whose records are visible; assignment-field scope controls which policy and assignment domains are visible. **Allow policies to grant this role** marks a safe role as automation-eligible.

Users can have several roles; effective permissions are their union. Root and protected high-privilege capabilities remain outside policy automation.
