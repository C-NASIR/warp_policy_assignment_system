The directory lists employees within the user's employee scope. Search matches visible employee details; department and employment-type filters narrow that list. **Clear filters** restores the current visible population.

Select a row to open the employee profile and assignments. **Add employee** appears only with creation authority. A missing employee may be outside your scope rather than absent from the system.
