The group directory lists explicit collections. A group detail page shows its description, members, and attached policies.

Adding or removing a member uses an assignment-impact preview. Attaching or detaching a policy is a distinct immediate action that reconciles current members. An attached effective policy becomes a candidate for every member without checking its direct conditions.

The same policy is deduplicated when direct and group paths overlap. Explanations identify the selected origin.
