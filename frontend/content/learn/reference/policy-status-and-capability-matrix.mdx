| Status | Participates in resolution | Typical available transition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | No | Activate when the record capability allows |
| Active | Yes, through its effective version | Create version or archive when allowed |
| Archived | No | Reactivate when allowed |

The policy detail response is authoritative for `can_create_version`, `can_activate`, and `can_archive`. Each capability combines the user's permission, employee/assignment-field scope, record status, and other safety rules.

Automated-access content can turn a lifecycle or version action into a previewed, independently approved workflow. Status changes preserve policy and assignment history.
