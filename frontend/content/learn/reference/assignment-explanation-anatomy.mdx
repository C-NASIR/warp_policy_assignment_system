## Policy source

A policy-derived explanation identifies the stable policy and exact version that supplied the value.

## Origin and evidence

A direct match records the clauses and trusted employee facts that matched. A group origin identifies the explicit group that contributed the policy. The same policy is considered only once when it applies through multiple origins.

## Selection

Selection evidence identifies the assignment field, its cardinality, the resolution strategy, the winning priority, and competing candidates with their outcomes.

## Override source

An override explanation identifies the immutable override record and the policy assignments it replaced. Updating or removing an override retires the old source so historical assignments can still refer to it.

The explanation is an immutable snapshot. A material explanation change can create a new history row even when the visible value remains the same.
