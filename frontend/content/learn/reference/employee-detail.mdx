The profile area shows trusted workforce inputs and the reporting relationship. Edit capability depends on `employees:update`.

**Current assignments** lists resolved field values. Expand a card to inspect policy/version source, direct evidence or group origin, priority, selection, and override replacement. Assignment-field scope can hide fields even when the employee is visible.

**Manual overrides** supports reviewed exceptions with `assignments:manage`. **Assignment history** includes current and retired rows with their source and effective interval.
