## People and data

**Employee**  
A workforce record evaluated by policies. It can hold trusted facts, group memberships, assignments, and history without having login access.

**User account**  
A sign-in identity with credentials, roles, permissions, scopes, and security settings. It may be linked to an employee.

**Condition field**  
A trusted static or supported derived employee fact that a policy condition can test, such as state, department, start date, tenure, or manager relationship.

**Assignment field**  
A named policy output with fixed `one` or `many` cardinality, such as Pay schedule or Application access.

**Assignment value**  
A value a policy version proposes for an assignment field, or the value an override explicitly supplies.

## Policies and matching

**Policy**  
The stable named identity of a rule. Its status determines whether its effective version can participate in assignment resolution.

**Policy version**  
The dated executable definition containing conditions, output values, priority, effective dates, and optional eligible automated roles.

**Effective date**  
A calendar boundary that determines when a policy version can participate. Policy-version start and end dates are inclusive.

**Match**  
A result showing that an employee satisfied a policy version's direct conditions. Matching makes the policy a candidate; it does not guarantee that every value will be selected.

**Group**  
An explicit set of employees to which policies can be attached. An attached effective policy becomes a candidate for every member even if its direct conditions do not match.

**Origin**  
How a policy reached an employee: either a direct condition match or inheritance through an explicit group.

## Resolution and results

**Candidate**  
A policy-supplied value eligible for assignment-field resolution after status, effective date, and direct or group applicability are considered.

**Cardinality**  
The assignment field's resolution shape. `one` selects one winning value; `many` retains a set of unique values.

**Priority**  
The policy-version number used to select among candidates for a one-value field and to choose provenance when duplicate many-field values have multiple sources. Higher numbers win.

**Conflict**  
An unresolved condition where different values tie at the highest priority for a one-value field. PolicyOS stops instead of picking arbitrarily.

**Assignment**  
The resolved value for one employee and assignment field, with its exact source, effective interval, and explanation snapshot.

**Override**  
An explicit employee-and-field exception applied after policy resolution. It replaces all policy-derived values for that field and preserves its own provenance.

## Safe change and evidence

**Preview**  
A non-persistent simulation of a proposed connected change and its expected impact.

**Approval request**  
A persisted request for independent review of an exact supported sensitive change. Not every preview creates one.

**Execution**  
The act that commits the exact approved operation. Approval by itself does not change production state.

**Reconciliation**  
Downstream recomputation of assignments and eligible policy-derived roles after relevant committed changes.

**Assignment history**  
The temporal record used to answer what assignment value and source were true for an employee at a time. Its timestamp intervals are half-open.

**Audit log**  
The append-only action record used to answer what changed, when, and by whom. Audit timestamps are shown in UTC.

**Provenance**  
The recorded source and evidence that explain where a value came from, including the policy version, origin, selection, or immutable override record.
