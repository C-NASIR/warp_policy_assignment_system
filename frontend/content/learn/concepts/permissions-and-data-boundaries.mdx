## Action permission is one check

A permission such as `employees:read` or `policies:create` allows an action category. Human access is the union of allow-only roles; roles do not contain deny rules.

## Scope is another check

Employee scope independently limits whose records are visible: none, the linked employee, the linked employee and reporting tree, or all employees. Assignment-field scope independently limits which assignment and policy domains are visible: none, selected fields, or all fields.

Two users with the same action permission can therefore see different employees or assignment fields. A direct request for an out-of-scope resource may return 404 so its existence is not disclosed.

When diagnosing access, check all three dimensions: action permission, employee scope, and assignment-field scope. Also verify the user-to-employee link when self or reporting-tree scope is involved.
