## Direct origin

A direct match means the employee satisfied the effective policy version's condition tree. The explanation records the matching clauses and employee facts.

## Group origin

A group-inherited policy reaches the employee because three explicit relationships exist:

1. the employee is a member of the group;
2. the policy is attached to the group; and
3. the policy has an effective version that can participate.

The employee does not also need to satisfy that policy's direct condition tree. The explanation names the group as the origin.

Groups do not compute membership from department, location, or another field. If Avery belongs to Engineering, that is an explicit membership—not an inference from `department = Engineering`.

## Deduplication

The same policy may reach an employee through a direct match and one or more groups. PolicyOS deduplicates those origins before resolution, so the policy's values are not counted repeatedly.

Membership changes are previewed. Attaching or detaching a policy is a distinct group action that reconciles affected members immediately.
