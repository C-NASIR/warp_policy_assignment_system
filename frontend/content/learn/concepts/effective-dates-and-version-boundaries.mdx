## Inclusive policy dates

A policy version's start and end dates are inclusive. A version dated September 1 through September 30 can participate on both boundary dates.

Versions within the same policy cannot overlap. Gaps are allowed, but the policy supplies no behavior during a gap.

## Scheduling a successor

Suppose California Pay Schedule version 1 is open-ended and assigns Bi-weekly. Creating version 2 with a start date of October 1, 2026 and Weekly pay automatically closes version 1 on September 30.

| Evaluation date | Effective version | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September 30, 2026 | Version 1 | Bi-weekly |
| October 1, 2026 | Version 2 | Weekly |

This boundary differs from assignment history, whose timestamp intervals are half-open: a history row is valid at `valid_from` and stops immediately before `valid_to`.

For today, PolicyOS reads persisted current assignments. A future-date query calculates the prospective result without persisting it; a past query reads recorded history. Do not assume that viewing a future answer has reconciled current state.
