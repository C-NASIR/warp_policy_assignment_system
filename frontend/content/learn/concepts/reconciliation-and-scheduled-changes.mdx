## What reconciliation does

Reconciliation reevaluates affected employees after a committed employee, policy, group, or override change. It updates current assignments, closes and opens history rows when material results change, and synchronizes eligible policy-derived roles for linked accounts.

The affected population depends on the mutation. Editing Avery's state recalculates Avery. Activating a policy can affect every employee who now matches. Attaching a policy to a group reconciles the group's members immediately.

Future policy versions participate when their effective boundary arrives. The application does not currently expose a general scheduled-operation UI, so documentation should describe the resulting behavior without promising a control that is not present.

Preview estimates impact before commitment; reconciliation produces the persisted downstream state after commitment. Do not treat them as the same event.
