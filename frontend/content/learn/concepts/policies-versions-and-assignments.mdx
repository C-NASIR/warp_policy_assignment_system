## Policy

A policy is the stable named identity, such as **California Pay Schedule**. Its status controls whether it participates in resolution: draft policies do not affect assignments, active policies can, and archived policies do not.

## Policy version

A version is the executable definition. It contains the condition tree, priority, effective dates, assignment values, and any eligible automated roles. PolicyOS chooses the one version effective on the evaluation date. Effective-date ranges are inclusive, may contain gaps, and cannot overlap within one policy.

## Assignment

An assignment is the resolved employee result, such as **Pay schedule: Bi-weekly**. It is not an editable copy of the policy. It records exactly one source policy version or manual override, an effective timestamp interval, and an explanation snapshot.

Keeping these objects separate lets a policy evolve without rewriting the evidence for past employee results.
