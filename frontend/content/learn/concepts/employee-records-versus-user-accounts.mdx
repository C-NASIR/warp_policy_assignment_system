## Employee record

An employee is the subject of policy evaluation. The record contains workforce facts, reporting relationships, group memberships, assignments, and assignment history. It does not need login credentials.

This separation lets PolicyOS represent people whose assignments matter even when they never operate the application.

## User account

A user is a sign-in identity with credentials, security state, explicit roles, and data scopes. Its permissions control which application actions and records the person can access.

An account can be linked to an employee. That link enables an eligible automated role from a matched policy to reach the correct sign-in identity. A missing link prevents that automated role grant; it does not prevent the employee's ordinary assignment calculation.

Explicit account roles and policy-derived roles have separate provenance. Reconciliation may add or remove the derived role, but it preserves explicit role assignments.

## A reliable diagnostic

When a workforce assignment is missing, investigate the employee facts and policies. When sign-in access or an automated role is missing, also check the user account, employee link, and the role's automation eligibility.
