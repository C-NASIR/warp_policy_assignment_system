## Two sources of role membership

An explicit role is assigned directly to a user by an access administrator. A policy-derived role comes from an effective policy that applies to the employee linked to that user.

Automated assignment requires three things: the role is eligible for automation, the policy applies to the employee, and the employee is linked to the account. Sensitive policy changes that grant automated human access require independent approval and exact execution.

Reconciliation maintains only the derived source. If a policy stops applying, its derived role can be removed while an explicit assignment of that same role remains. This source separation prevents policy automation from erasing deliberate administrative access.

Highly privileged authorities are not eligible for policy automation. Use explicit least-privilege administration for those responsibilities.
