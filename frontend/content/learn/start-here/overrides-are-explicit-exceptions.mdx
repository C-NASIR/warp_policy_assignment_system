## Replacement, not another candidate

A manual override is an explicit exception for one employee and one assignment field. It is applied after policy resolution and replaces all policy-derived results for that field.

Avery normally has **Pay schedule: Bi-weekly** from California Pay Schedule. An assignment manager previews an override to **Monthly**, then commits it. Avery's current Pay schedule becomes Monthly; Bi-weekly remains visible in the override explanation as a replaced policy result.

Removing the override does not require recreating the policy assignment. Reconciliation resolves the field again, so Bi-weekly returns from the still-matching California policy.

## Provenance survives change

Updating or removing an override retires its immutable source record instead of erasing it. Assignment history and audit evidence can therefore identify which exception produced a past value.

An override is not a conflict repair. Policy candidates are resolved before the override is applied, so an equal-priority conflict between different one-field values still stops resolution. Fix the rule overlap or priorities first.

Use overrides for intentional, reviewable exceptions—not as a hidden substitute for maintaining the policies.
