## Reconstruct the result

Start with Avery Chen's trusted facts: California, Engineering, Full-time, San Francisco, start date September 14, 2026, and manager Maya Patel. The employee record can receive assignments before the separate user account is linked.

On the evaluation date, PolicyOS chooses each active policy's effective version. Direct conditions make the California, Full-time, and Engineering policies candidates. Explicit Engineering group membership also supplies Engineering Access even though its direct Remote-location condition does not match Avery.

The engine resolves each assignment field:

| Field | Candidate reasoning | Final result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pay schedule (one) | Bi-weekly at priority 20 beats Semi-monthly at 10 | Bi-weekly |
| Vacation policy (one) | Standard PTO is the winning candidate | Standard PTO |
| Application access (many) | Union of direct 1Password and group-origin GitHub and Linear | 1Password, GitHub, Linear |
| Compliance training (many) | California Compliance contributes one unique value | CA Workplace Harassment |
| Equipment stipend (one) | Engineering Equipment supplies the value | $1,000 annual |

Each result stores an explanation of its policy version, origin, evidence, and selection. A manual override to Monthly would replace the Pay schedule policy result after resolution; removing it would let Bi-weekly return.

## Continue the timeline

California Pay Schedule version 2 starts October 1, 2026 and supplies Weekly. Because version dates are inclusive, version 1 ends September 30. A sensitive Engineering Access version that grants an automated role is previewed, independently approved, and executed before reconciliation can grant that eligible role to Avery's linked account.

Assignment history answers what value was true over time. Audit answers what changed and who caused it.

## Check your understanding

<details>
<summary>Avery does not satisfy Engineering Access's direct condition. Why do GitHub and Linear still apply?</summary>

Avery is an explicit member of the Engineering group, and Engineering Access is attached to that group. Group inheritance makes the policy a candidate without requiring a direct condition match.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why does Semi-monthly not become Avery's Pay schedule even though US Employee Pay matches?</summary>

Pay schedule is a one-value field. California Pay Schedule supplies Bi-weekly at priority 20, which beats Semi-monthly at priority 10.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where would you look for Avery's past value, and where would you look for the actor who changed it?</summary>

Use assignment history for the past value and its source. Use the audit log for the actor, action, and change timestamp.

</details>

You are ready when you can explain every final value with this chain: **employee fact or group → effective policy version → candidate → cardinality and priority → assignment explanation**.
