## Triage by risk

Open **Access review**. Start with privileged active users lacking MFA, then investigate stale users, roles with broad employee or assignment-field scope, and roles with no current users.

Open the relevant access or account-security destination for remediation. Confirm business ownership before disabling a user or narrowing a role because shared role changes affect every assigned user.

Re-run the review after remediation and preserve supporting audit evidence. A finding is a signal to investigate, not proof that access is unauthorized.
