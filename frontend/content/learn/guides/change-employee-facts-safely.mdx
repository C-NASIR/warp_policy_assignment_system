## Edit the source fact

Open the employee and choose the profile edit action. Change only facts supported by your source record—for example a department transfer, new location, or manager change.

Choose **Review assignments** and compare added, changed, and unchanged values. Derived organization and tenure conditions can make one fact affect more than the obvious policy.

## Confirm the impact

If the preview is correct, choose **Confirm changes**. Reconciliation updates the employee's current assignments and preserves retired results in history. Then expand changed assignments to verify their new source and evidence.

If a result is surprising, cancel and investigate policy status, effective version, conditions, groups, priority, and overrides before retrying.
