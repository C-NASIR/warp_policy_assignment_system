## Open the employee

Open **Employees**, find the employee, and select their record. Under **Current assignments**, locate the assignment field and value you are investigating.

## Expand the assignment

Open the assignment card. PolicyOS identifies whether the source is a policy or a manual override. For a policy result, inspect the policy name, condition evidence or group origin, and priority. For an override, inspect the policy value it replaced.

## Compare match and selection

A policy can match an employee without supplying the final assignment. For a one-value field, a higher-priority candidate can win. For a many-value field, unique results are combined. A manual override replaces all policy-derived values for its field.

If the source still seems unexpected, open the policy and confirm its status, effective version, condition tree, output field, and impact summary.
