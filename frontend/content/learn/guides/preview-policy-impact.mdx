## Inspect two populations

First review who matches the direct conditions or receives the policy through a group. Then review which assignment values are selected after priority, cardinality, competing policies, and overrides.

A high match count does not mean the policy supplies every final value. Look for one-field winners, many-field additions, unchanged results, conflicts, and automated-role changes.

Change any input to the proposal only before previewing again. Execution is tied to the exact connected preview; never assume a stale impact report authorizes modified input.
