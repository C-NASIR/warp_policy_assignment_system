## Create the account

Open **Access control**, select **Users**, and choose **Create user**. Enter the person's name, unique email, and a temporary password of at least 12 characters. Assign at least one least-privilege explicit role.

If you can read the intended employee, select it under **Linked employee**. Self and reporting-tree scopes use this link as their starting point, and policy-derived roles require it. Leave the account unlinked when no workforce relationship is intended.

Save the user and deliver the temporary credential through your approved secure process. New users must change it at first sign-in. Verify explicit and policy-derived roles separately after reconciliation.
