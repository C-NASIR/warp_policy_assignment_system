## Review independently

Open **Approvals** and select a pending request created by another user. Verify the author, operation, exact payload, affected records, assignment impact, and automated-access changes. Reject it with a useful reason when the intent or scope is wrong.

The author cannot approve their own request. Available buttons also depend on request status, expiry, and request-specific capabilities.

## Approve, then execute

Approval records authorization but does not commit the mutation. After approving, choose **Execute** as the approving user. PolicyOS verifies the exact request and reconciles downstream state.

If the request is stale, expired, or needs editing, do not reuse it. Ask the author to preview the current proposal and obtain a new approval.
