## Check the blast radius

Before attaching a policy, review its status, effective version, outputs, and the group's full member list. Every member receives the attached policy as a candidate regardless of direct condition match.

Use the group's policy attachment control and confirm the action. Unlike membership edits, attachment and detachment currently reconcile members immediately rather than showing the membership preview flow.

Afterward, inspect a sample of affected employees and confirm the group origin, selection, and any removed values. Detaching one origin may leave the value in place when another direct or group source still supplies it.
