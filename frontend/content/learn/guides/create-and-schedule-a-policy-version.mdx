## Start from the policy record

Open the policy and choose its new-version action. Your record capability must allow version creation; a general permission does not override status or scope restrictions.

Copy only the behavior that should continue, then update conditions, outputs, priority, automated roles, and dates. A new start date after an open-ended version closes the prior version on the preceding day. Overlaps are rejected; gaps intentionally produce no policy behavior.

## Preview the exact version

Review affected employees, assignments, and access. If the active policy change includes automated access, submit it for independent approval. Otherwise, execute with the connected preview token when authorized.

After creation, confirm the version boundary and whether it is current or scheduled.
