## Create an explicit collection

Open **Groups**, choose the create action, and enter a clear name and description. Membership is manual: a group named Engineering does not automatically include everyone whose department is Engineering.

Open the group to manage members and attached policies. Preview membership additions or removals because they can change every assignment supplied through the group. Policy attachment and detachment are separate actions and reconcile current members immediately.

Periodically compare the group's stated purpose, member list, and attached policies. Remove stale membership rather than relying on a policy's direct conditions to cancel group inheritance.
