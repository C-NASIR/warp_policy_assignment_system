## Use record capabilities

Open the policy detail page. Available lifecycle actions reflect both your scope and the record's current status. Activate a draft to let its effective version participate; archive an active policy to remove it from resolution; reactivate an archived policy only when the record exposes that capability.

Ordinary lifecycle changes reconcile affected assignments after confirmation. A lifecycle change involving automated access uses preview and independent approval before execution.

After the action, verify policy status, current-version dates, impact, and a sample employee explanation. Archiving removes current participation but does not rewrite historical assignments or audit events.
