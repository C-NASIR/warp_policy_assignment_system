## Preview membership impact

Open the group and select the membership control. Choose an employee to add, or the remove action beside an existing member. Review the preview before confirming.

The preview should identify assignments added, changed, or removed by every attached policy. Remember that an attached policy applies to a member even when its direct conditions do not match. The same policy is deduplicated if another origin also applies.

Confirm only when the population and results are expected. Then open the employee and inspect changed assignment origins. Group membership changes are audited.
