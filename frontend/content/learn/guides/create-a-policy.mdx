## Define the rule

Open **Policies** and choose **Create policy**. Enter a stable policy name, priority, and effective dates. Build the ALL/ANY condition tree from available fields, then add one or more assignment-field values.

Use priority to express intentional specificity, not as a substitute for clear conditions. Check every output field's cardinality. Add only automation-eligible roles when the policy truly controls human access.

## Review and create

Use the impact panel to inspect matched employees and expected outputs. The initial-policy population count is an estimate; connected version previews use the authoritative change-preview contract.

Create the policy when the definition is correct. Depending on your authority and automated-access content, it may be active immediately or created as a draft for activation review. Verify the resulting status and assignments.
