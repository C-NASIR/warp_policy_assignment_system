## Enroll MFA

Open **Account security**. Enter your current password, choose **Set up authenticator**, add the displayed key to a TOTP authenticator, and verify a six-digit code. Save the recovery codes immediately; they are shown only once.

## Protect sessions

Use **Confirm your identity** when a sensitive administrative action requires recent reauthentication. Review active devices and choose **Sign out others** or **Sign out all** when a session is unfamiliar or no longer needed.

Changing your password signs out other active sessions. Review security events and acknowledge non-informational events only after investigation. Disabling MFA requires both the current password and an authenticator code.
