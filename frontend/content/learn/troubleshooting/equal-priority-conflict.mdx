Confirm the affected assignment field has `one` cardinality. List every candidate at the highest priority and compare values. Different values at that priority are ambiguous; identical values are not.

Give the intentionally more specific policy a higher priority, narrow overlapping conditions, or align the intended value. Then preview again across the full affected population.

Do not add an override to hide the error. Overrides apply only after policy resolution succeeds.
