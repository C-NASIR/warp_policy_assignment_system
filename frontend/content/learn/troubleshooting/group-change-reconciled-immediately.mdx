Identify which action occurred. Adding or removing a member uses a change preview. Attaching or detaching a policy is a distinct action that reconciles existing members immediately.

Check the audit event, affected group membership, and attached-policy list. Then inspect employee explanations to see which group origin disappeared or appeared.

A value can remain unchanged when another direct or group source still supplies it; its explanation or history can still change materially.
