Ask an access administrator to check:

1. the action permission for the route or operation;
2. employee visibility—none, self, reporting tree, or all;
3. assignment-field visibility—none, selected, or all; and
4. the account's employee link when self or reporting-tree scope is used.

Two users with the same read permission may see different records. A direct request outside scope can return 404 to avoid disclosing that the resource exists; do not interpret every 404 as deletion.
