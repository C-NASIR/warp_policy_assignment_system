Expand the assignment and look for a group origin. Then open that group and confirm the employee is an explicit member and the policy is attached.

An attached effective policy applies to every member without requiring its direct condition tree to match. This is expected group behavior, not a false condition result.

If the result is unwanted, correct explicit membership or attachment after reviewing the full affected population. Do not edit an unrelated condition expecting it to cancel group inheritance.
