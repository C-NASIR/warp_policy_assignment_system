Demo mode intentionally keeps mutations in local component state or displays a simulated success message. Refreshing reloads the curated sample data.

Employee and initial-policy previews use simplified browser-side calculations. Approval actions are disabled and no persisted approval queue exists. Demo assignments are examples, not a complete recomputation of every sample relationship.

Use demo mode to learn labels and navigation. Use a connected test workspace to verify persistence, authoritative previews, approval separation, reconciliation, history, or audit evidence.
