Now express a rule with a narrower population:

> Give the design tool to full-time employees in Design or Engineering.

Use a separate training policy called **Design Tools** so Standard Vacation keeps its original purpose.

1. Open **Create policy**, name the policy, and choose its priority and effective date.
2. Set the outer **Employees matching** selector to **ALL**.
3. Make the outer condition **Employee type = Full-time**.
4. Choose **Add nested group** and set that group's selector to **ANY**.
5. Set its first condition to **Department = Design**.
6. Choose **Add nested condition** and add **Department = Engineering**.
7. Under **What gets assigned**, choose the many-value **Application access** field and enter **Design tool**.

Read the structure aloud: full-time **AND** (Design **OR** Engineering). Rachel matches because she is full-time and belongs to Design. A part-time designer doesn't match.

You can also use derived inputs. **Tenure >= 2 years** describes completed calendar service based on the employee's start date and the evaluation date. You don't enter a separate tenure value on Rachel's employee record. Manager-related inputs are also available through the condition catalog.

The visual builder supports multiple sibling groups and up to three group levels total: the outer group plus two nested levels. Every group can independently match **ALL** or **ANY** of its conditions and child groups. The editor preserves the complete tree when you create a new version, but it has no general NOT control.

To keep rules reviewable and evaluation bounded, one policy can contain at most 20 groups and 50 conditions. Nested logic is compiled into at most 200 OR-of-AND evaluation clauses. The counters below the builder show current usage, and the API enforces the same limits for every client.

Choose **Preview impact** before saving, following the previous lesson's save and status checks. The connected engine evaluates derived facts such as completed-calendar tenure and reporting relationships. Pay special attention around anniversaries and indirect reporting relationships, then confirm the persisted result after saving.

If you open **New version** on an existing policy instead, previewing follows the connected version-review workflow described later. The complete supported condition tree is prefilled; inspect it and preview the resulting population before saving.
